The tail is where shared systems reveal themselves

Percentiles
Mean latency answers “what happened on average?” p95 and p99 ask “what experience did the unlucky requests receive?”

Report

sample count, p50/p95/p99, failures, cancellations and timeouts

Do not infer

a reliable p99 from a tiny sample or from omitted failures

Percentiles need adequate sample size and a defined population. Failed requests must not disappear from the report. With 20 samples, p99 is essentially the maximum and should not be presented as a stable tail estimate.
