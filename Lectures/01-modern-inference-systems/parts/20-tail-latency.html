Mean latency tells the average story. p95 and p99 reveal the experience of unlucky requests.
Report sample count, p50/p95/p99, failures, cancellations and timeouts. Never infer a stable p99 from a tiny sample.
Percentiles need adequate sample size and a defined population. Failed requests must not disappear from the report. With 20 samples, p99 is essentially the maximum and should not be presented as a stable tail estimate.
