Near saturation, a small load increase can create a large wait

Queueing

Below the knee

Throughput rises; queues clear; latency stays controlled.

Beyond the knee

Throughput flattens while queue time, tail latency and timeouts rise.

Capacity is the highest offered load that still satisfies the full SLO.

Avoid claiming a universal utilization threshold. The knee depends on service-time variability, scheduler, workload and SLO. The correct boundary is empirical and contractual.
