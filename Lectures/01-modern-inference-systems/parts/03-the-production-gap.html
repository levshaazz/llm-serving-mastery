A model that runs is not yet a service

The gap
“It answered once” proves functional correctness for one input. Production asks whether the system keeps a measurable promise across users, time and failures.
Shift the question: from “How fast is the model?” to “Which workload can this service sustain while meeting its SLO?”
Let students contrast a notebook demo with a product endpoint. Elicit concurrency, cancellation, observability, versioning and failure isolation before revealing later slides.
