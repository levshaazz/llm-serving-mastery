Data plane and control plane fail differently

Operational boundary

Data plane

Carries live inference requests, tokens and cancellations. Its SLO is user-facing.

Control plane

Configures models, replicas, rollouts, placement and policy. Its mistakes change the data plane.

A healthy control API does not prove that generation works; a working replica does not prove that rollout policy is safe.

This prepares students for Kubernetes and model deployment later without teaching infrastructure prematurely. Ask for separate readiness checks for each plane.
