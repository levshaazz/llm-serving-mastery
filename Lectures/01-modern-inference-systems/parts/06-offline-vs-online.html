Offline inference and online serving optimize different objectives

Modes

Offline / batch

  • Finite known dataset
  • Throughput and total cost dominate
  • Delay can be amortized
  • Failures can often be retried later

Online / interactive

  • Arrivals are uncertain
  • TTFT and tail latency matter
  • Users cancel and retry
  • Overload must be controlled now
The same engine can support both modes, but a benchmark must say which problem it models. Throughput-only conclusions from an offline batch are not evidence for an interactive SLO.
