Little’s Law is a consistency check, not a capacity oracle

Reasoning tool
$$L = \lambda W$$
\(L\)
average requests in the system
\(\lambda\)
long-run arrival rate in a stable system
\(W\)
average time in the system
Example: 8 completed requests/s with 0.5 s mean time implies about 4 requests in the chosen system boundary. It does not predict GPU capacity, p95, or behavior in an unstable growing queue.
