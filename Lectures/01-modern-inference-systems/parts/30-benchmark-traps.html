Common results that do not support their conclusion

Critical reading
ClaimMissing control
“A is 2× faster than B”same model revision, tokens, hardware, load and quality
“p99 is 300 ms”sample size, failures and percentile method
“supports 128 users”arrival model and SLO at 128
“uses 10 GB”idle/peak, allocated/reserved, context and concurrency
Invite students to repair each statement into a falsifiable claim. This is the critical-evaluation habit they should apply to vendor benchmarks and their own leaderboard entries.
