Production failure is broader than “the process crashed”

Failure modes
LayerExamplesEvidence
Contractwrong schema, truncated stream, ignored parameterprotocol test
PerformanceSLO miss, queue runaway, starvationclient latency + queue metrics
ResourceOOM, fragmentation, disk exhaustionruntime and host telemetry
Lifecyclecold start, bad rollout, partial readinessevents + health probes
A 200 response can still be a failure if it violates the declared contract. Conversely, a deliberate 429 under overload may be healthy behavior if admission control is part of the contract.
