Exercise: turn a product sentence into an experiment

8 minutes
“Serve an assistant to 50 simultaneous users with fast responses on one GPU.”
  1. Define the SLO and failure behavior.
  2. Specify token distributions and arrival model.
  3. Choose timestamps and raw audit artifacts.
Have pairs produce a compact contract. Debrief by showing that “50 simultaneous users” alone is underspecified: think time, input/output length, stream expectations and SLO all matter.
