Open with the diagnostic promise: we will not memorize “decode is memory-bound”; we will calculate when that statement is plausible and how to falsify it.
The stove is ready. The ingredients are not.

A GPU can have abundant arithmetic and still wait for bytes. The first task is to name which bytes, from where, and how often.

The pantry is device memory; the stove is arithmetic. Keep the analogy strict: it illustrates movement, not exact GPU topology.

A bottleneck is the active constraint for a defined workload and phase. Several constraints can appear sequentially.

A percentage is not a diagnosis

What does “GPU 99%” mean?
Common inference

“Utilization is high, therefore compute is saturated.”

A busy interval may contain memory stalls, tiny kernels, late work, or alternating phases. Diagnose with operations, bytes, shapes, timelines, and counters.
NVML-style utilization commonly answers whether kernels were active during sampling windows, not whether tensor cores reached a peak.

Every hypothesis starts with four quantities

Dimension the problem
PERFORMANCE ACCOUNTING
Operations · bytes moved · live bytes · elapsed time

Operations and traffic predict rate ceilings; live bytes predict fit; elapsed time tells whether the chosen interval includes queueing, transfers, or asynchronous work.

Make students say units: FLOP, byte, byte/s, FLOP/s, seconds. Unit errors are often conceptual errors.
