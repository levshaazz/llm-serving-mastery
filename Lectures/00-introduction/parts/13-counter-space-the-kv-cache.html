Keys and values, for every layer, every KV head — kept for every token of every live request.

The real limit

Batching is free in bandwidth, but not in memory. Quantization, paging and cache reuse exist to buy counter space.

The arithmetic: 2 (K and V) × 36 layers × 8 KV heads × 128 head dim × 2 bytes = 147 456 bytes ≈ 144 KiB per token. With ~6 GB left after weights and runtime overhead: 6·10⁹ / 147 456 ≈ 40 700 tokens in total, ≈ 636 per request at 64 concurrent requests — prompt plus answer. That is why the leaderboard rule says "the model plus its KV cache for 64 concurrent requests must fit in 16 GB". Every lever you will pull — quantized weights, quantized KV cache, PagedAttention's no-waste allocation, prefix caching — is a way to buy counter space. The architecture numbers are a representative 4B-class configuration; your model's config.json gives its own.
