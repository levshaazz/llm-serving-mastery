The fix, in one picture

Continuous batching: one wok, many orders. A new order joins at the next toss; a finished plate leaves at the next toss. Nobody waits for the whole table.

Preview of topics 4–7. Static batching waits until a batch is full and until its longest answer is done. Continuous (iteration-level) batching re-forms the batch at every decode step: requests enter and leave the running batch individually (Orca, OSDI 2022; vLLM). PagedAttention (vLLM, SOSP 2023) is the counter split into small fixed trays, so no guest reserves space they never use. We do not explain them today — we just watch the effect.
