How a round is scored

Quality is a gate, speed is the race
StepRule
ContractServer comes up and lists submission within the start-up window (TBA) — else the round is 0.
Quality gateSubset of GSM8K, MMLU-Pro and IFEval via lm-eval ≥ the gate (TBA) — else the round is 0.
SpeedOutput tok/s at concurrency 32 and 64 + p95 TTFT, GuideLLM on a fixed hidden prompt set → rank.
Mark 0–5Clear the round's speed bar → 5 / 5. The bar rises every round (baseline + x%, TBA).
OverdriveTop 10% of the round +2, next 10% +1.
Exam waiverOverdrive ≥ N (TBA) after round 10 → final exam waived, credited at 40 / 50.
Read it top to bottom. A server that does not start, or that got fast by getting dumb, scores zero for the round — the quality gate stops quantizing a model into nonsense. Among valid submissions, speed decides. The bar that gives a full 5 rises round by round, so standing still is not enough. Overdrive rewards those who are consistently at the top: it does not raise your 40% above 40, it buys the right to skip the final exam. Exact bars, the gate thresholds, N and the partial-credit formula are published with the leaderboard spec.
