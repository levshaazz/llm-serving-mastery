How a round is scored

Gates first, then one number: S
StepRule
SnapshotThursday 23:59 MSK: the commit your round-NN tag points to. No tag → 0.
ContractServer starts and lists submission within 30 min; a streamed smoke answer works.
Quality gateGSM8K, IFEval, MMLU-Pro subsets (lm-eval): each ≥ 90% of the reference.
Latency gatep95 TTFT at 64 clients ≤ the round's SLO; errors ≤ 1%; answers really 256 tokens.
Same modelHidden canary prompts, answered at rest and again under load, must match.
Speed\(S = \sqrt{\text{tok/s}_{32} \cdot \text{tok/s}_{64}}\)
Mark 0–5\(5 \times \min(1,\ S / S_{\text{bar}})\); any failed gate → 0. Round = 4%: points = mark × 0.8.
Top to bottom, every round. The snapshot makes the deadline real. A server that does not start, or that got fast by getting dumb, scores zero — the quality gate stops quantizing a model into nonsense, the latency gate stops buying throughput with a 30-second first token, the output-length check stops servers that ignore max_tokens / ignore_eos. Among valid submissions only S matters: the geometric mean of throughput at 32 and 64 clients, so you cannot win on one level and collapse on the other. The mark is proportional: half the bar = 2.5 of 5. Each round is worth 4% of the course grade (mark × 0.8); ten rounds = 40%.
