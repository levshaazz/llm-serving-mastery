How to succeed

Prerequisites · habits

Bring

  • ·Python, confidently; asyncio basics help.
  • ·Linux command line and git; uv is shown in seminar 1.
  • ·Deep-learning basics: tokens, a transformer forward pass, PyTorch at the reading level.
  • ·HTTP / REST basics: requests, JSON, streaming.
  • ·Accounts: GitHub, Hugging Face, Google (Colab).

No Docker or Kubernetes experience needed — that is what topics 9–12 are for.

Habits that win

  • ✓Create your repository this week and submit round 1 early — a boring server that starts beats a clever one that does not.
  • ✓Change one thing per experiment; predict, then measure.
  • ✓Write the journal entry the day you run the experiment.
  • ✓Read the dry-run log every week.

Workload

≈ 10–14 hours a week: lecture, seminar, and your leaderboard repository.

The strongest predictor of a good leaderboard story is starting early and changing one variable at a time. The weakest: a big-bang rewrite the night before a deadline.
