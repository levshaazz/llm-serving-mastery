Illustrative numbers, not measurements — the live demo measures the real ones on a Colab T4. The point is the shape: with a serial server, the wait of the k-th client grows linearly in k, while the throughput stays flat. Adding clients adds only waiting, not work done. Ask: is the GPU busy during these 6.8 minutes? Hold the answer for two slides.
