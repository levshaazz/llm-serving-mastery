the premise of this course
A model that answers one prompt in two seconds on your laptop is a demo. A service that answers two hundred people at once, with a predictable first-token delay, without running out of memory, at a known cost per million tokens — that is what this course builds. The trail is the course in four stones: one stove, a better cooking system (vLLM), more stoves (multi-GPU), more kitchens (Kubernetes).
