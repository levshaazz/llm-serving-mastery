Policy

Cheating: zero tolerance. No late submissions — a missed round deadline is a 0 for that round. Gaming the benchmark (caching eval answers, special-casing the prompt set, degrading output to win speed) disqualifies the round.

The scale is standard. The leaderboard adds one rule of its own: you optimize the server, not the benchmark. The speed prompts are hidden and may rotate; anything that only works because you know the test is disqualified.
