What this means for you

Develop and debug on Colab; trust only the judge's numbers. Low-precision tricks (FP8, NVFP4) cannot be tested on a T4 — use the weekly dry-run logs to check that they start on Blackwell.

An optimization that wins on the T4 may not transfer, and vice versa: the judge has ~2.8× the memory bandwidth, and supports data types the T4 does not have at all. That is realistic — production hardware is rarely your laptop. Write down in your journal which results came from Colab and which from the judge.
