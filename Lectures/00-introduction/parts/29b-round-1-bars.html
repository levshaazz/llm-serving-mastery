Where they come from

The reference submission — Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct on plain vLLM — put through the judge on a Colab T4. Quality gates are 90% of its scores; the SLO is its p95 TTFT × 1.25.

They move every week

Gates, \(S_{\text{bar}}\) and the SLO are updated and published on the leaderboard page before each round opens. Last week's 5 / 5 is not this week's.

Round 1 is deliberately reachable: the bar was measured on a T4, and the judge's RTX 5070 Ti has about 2.8× its memory bandwidth, so the reference submission should clear it there with room to spare. Getting a working, reproducible server through the contract is the real task of week 1. From round 2 the bars are re-set every week and published with the round; the source of each week's bars is written next to them on the leaderboard page. Numbers: judge/thresholds/round-01.json.
