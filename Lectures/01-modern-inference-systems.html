The incident did not begin at the GPU

A plausible trace

What operators saw

  • TTFT p99 jumped from 0.7 s to 18 s.
  • GPU utilization stayed high.
  • Request attempts rose after timeouts.
  • Completed tokens increased; useful answers fell.

What actually changed

  • Arrival rate crossed sustainable admission rate.
  • Queue residence consumed the deadline.
  • Clients retried stale work.
  • Cancellation did not reach the scheduler.
These are illustrative incident numbers. The diagnostic structure, not the values, is the lesson.

Serving is a promise with a workload attached

Working definition
SYSTEM CONTRACT
A serving system accepts demand, schedules stateful generation, streams results, and sheds work so that declared quality, latency, throughput, cost, and availability objectives hold for a declared workload.

Remove the workload and the promise is unfalsifiable. Remove failure policy and the promise fails exactly when it matters.
