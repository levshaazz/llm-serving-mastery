## L02-01 — the memory wall

Use case: `illustration-story`. Generate an editorial hand-drawn 16:9 opening hero plate for an advanced
LLM inference engineering lecture. In a whimsical GPU kitchen, Serega stands between an enormous pantry
wall representing high-capacity memory and a small blazing stove representing compute. A long stream of
ingredient cards travels from the pantry to the stove for every serving, and the stove waits while the
conveyor is packed. Communicate data movement versus arithmetic without labels.

Preserve the locked Serega appearance from `_research/mascots.py`: long black hair, course-blue tunic,
exactly one forest-green Tatar tübetey, and no chef's toque. Use thick black ink on warm off-white paper,
course-blue fills, and small warm-orange accents. Green appears only on Serega's skullcap. Avoid text,
pseudo-text, logos, watermarks, gradients, shadows, 3D, photorealism, plants, repeated Serega, and any
second hat.
